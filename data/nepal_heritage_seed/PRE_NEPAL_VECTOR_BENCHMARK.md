# Pre-Nepal visual vector benchmark

Generated by `ml_pipeline/benchmarks/evaluate_retrieval.py`. Do not edit by hand.

## Method

Model `openai/clip-vit-base-patch32` (512-d, L2-normalized) on Apple `mps`. Every image is encoded as a center-biased pyramid in one batched forward pass: 0.35 full frame + 0.35 center 70% + 0.20 center 50% + 0.10 mirrored center 70%. The gallery is instance-level: each of the 354 photos keeps its own vector, and a photo that matches the CLIP cue prompt of a neighboring confuser (listed in `confusableWith` or within 40 m) better than its own is flagged (188 photos). The tuner set the weight of flagged photos to 1.00, which leaves them at full weight: downweighting did not help on the tuning split. 78 monuments are indexed, 9 of them zero-shot. Mislabeled Commons photos were removed or re-filed by eye before this run; `precomputed_index/harvest_manifest.json` lists each one with the reason.

Ranking has two stages, implemented once in `ml_pipeline/embeddings/geo_visual_ranker.py` and used by both this benchmark and the inference worker. Stage 1 keeps the monuments within 150 m of the GPS fix (all of them without a fix), scores each as 0.70 x its best photo + 0.30 x the mean of its best two, and passes the top 5 on. Stage 2 adds 0.00 x the z-scored CLIP similarity between the query and each candidate's contrastive cue prompt and 0.12 x a Gaussian proximity prior (sigma 25 m) on the distance from the fix to each candidate, both normalized across those 5 candidates. The query is never fused with its own label.

MRR counts the reciprocal rank inside the three returned matches, and zero when the monument is not among them.

## Tuning protocol

The Stage 2 weights, the noisy-photo downweight and the prior width were chosen by a grid search over 864 configurations that maximizes Recall@1 (ties: MRR, then the least intervention) on 285 leave-one-out queries taken only from photos other than each monument's first sorted photo. Those tuning queries use the standoff GPS model, so the prior is not fitted to a fix centered on the monument. The headline in (c) uses only the held-out first photos; the tuning split scored 0.856 and the held-out queries 0.892.

## Encode latency

Warm median **52.8 ms** per `/embed` call on `mps`, measured over 50 gallery photos through the worker's handler: base64 and JPEG decode, 512 px thumbnail, quality gate, the four-view CLIP encode and the two-stage ranking. HTTP transport is not included.

## Zero-shot coverage

These monuments have no reference photo. Their only gallery vector is the mean of four CLIP text prompts (roof, materials, guardians, disambiguation tip), which scores far below any photo match, so they surface only when no photographed monument is in range: Bone Chorten Gateways, Chini Lama Residence, Prayer Wheel Arcade, Changu Narayan Satellite Trail, Vasuki Nath Mandir, Dewa Dharma Monastery, Nagarkot Viewpoint Stele, Vajra Dhatu Mandala, Vasubandhu Chorten.

## Caveats

- Commons photos by the same uploader can remain in the gallery when one of them is the query. The near-duplicate guard removes re-uploads of one frame, not a second frame from the same visit.
- Monument coordinates are desk-research approximations. The geometric prior, and the simulated fix that is drawn around those same coordinates, need field-verified GPS before the prior's gain can be trusted on site.
- The tuning split and the held-out queries come from the same monuments and share gallery photos; only the query photos are disjoint.
- These first-photo queries are not the same files as in the earlier 0.625 run: curation removed or re-filed mislabeled photos, some of which were query photos, and newly added photos can sort first.
