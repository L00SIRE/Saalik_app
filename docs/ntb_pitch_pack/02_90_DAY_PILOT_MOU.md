# Memorandum of understanding: 90-day Saalik heritage pilot

**Status: draft for discussion. It is not legal advice and creates no obligation until both parties have had it reviewed and have signed it.**

## 1. Parties

- **Nepal Tourism Board** ("the Board"), Bhrikutimandap, Kathmandu.
- **[Saalik legal entity name and registered address]** ("Saalik").

## 2. Purpose

To test, over 90 days, whether Saalik's phone-based monument guide can (a) identify monuments reliably on site, (b) move visitors toward lesser-visited heritage towns, and (c) generate bookings and income for licensed local guides.

## 3. Term

The pilot starts on the date of the last signature below and runs for 90 days. It ends automatically unless both parties agree in writing to extend it.

## 4. Scope

| Item | Proposed scope |
| --- | --- |
| Zones | Patan Durbar Square and Kathmandu Durbar Square, plus Kirtipur, Bungamati and Panauti as the dispersion destinations. |
| Monuments | Every monument in Saalik's corpus within those zones. |
| Guides | Up to 20 guides holding a current licence, introduced by the Board. |
| Visitors | Any visitor who chooses to install the app. No visitor is required to use it. |

## 5. Schedule

| Days | Work | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 to 30 | Field photography of every pilot monument from five standard angles. Survey of each monument's GPS position. Review of the English and Nepali texts with site custodians. | Reference index built from field photographs. Verified texts and coordinates. |
| 31 to 60 | Guide onboarding. Public availability of the app in the pilot zones. | First weekly dispersion reports. First guide bookings. |
| 61 to 90 | Continued operation. Measurement of recognition accuracy on photographs taken on site and held out of the index. | Closing report and a joint recommendation on extension. |

## 6. Responsibilities of Saalik

1. Provide the mobile app, the command center and all engineering at its own cost.
2. Carry out the field photography and GPS survey, following each site's rules on photography and access.
3. Deliver a weekly dispersion report to the Board's point of contact.
4. Correct any monument text that a custodian or the Board identifies as inaccurate, within 7 days of being told.
5. Deliver the closing report within 14 days of the end of the term.

## 7. Responsibilities of the Board

1. Name one point of contact for the term.
2. Provide a letter of introduction to the custodians of the pilot monuments and, where a permit is needed for photography, support Saalik's application for it.
3. Introduce Saalik to licensed guides in the pilot zones.
4. Attend a review meeting once every 30 days.

## 8. Measures of success

Both parties will review the following at day 90. Targets will be agreed at the kickoff meeting and recorded as an annex.

1. Recognition accuracy: the share of on-site photographs, held out of the index, for which the correct monument is the first result and for which it is among the first three.
2. Dispersion: the share of all scans recorded in Kirtipur, Bungamati and Panauti, by week.
3. Guide outcomes: the number of bookings that began with a scan, and the income guides report from them.
4. Content quality: the number of monument texts reviewed by a custodian, and the number of corrections made.

## 9. Data

1. Saalik will share scan data with the Board only in aggregate: counts by zone, monument and hour. It will not share any data that identifies an individual visitor.
2. Saalik collects a visitor's location only while the visitor is using the scanner, and uses it only to identify the monument.
3. The verified monument texts and surveyed coordinates produced during the pilot may be used by both parties without restriction.
4. Field photographs taken by Saalik remain Saalik's property. Saalik grants the Board a non-exclusive licence to use them for heritage promotion, with credit.
5. Each party will handle personal data in accordance with the laws of Nepal.

## 10. Cost

Neither party pays the other under this memorandum. Each party bears its own costs. Guides keep the tips and fees they receive from visitors during the pilot; Saalik takes no share of them during the term.

## 11. Use of names

Saalik may describe the pilot as "a pilot with the Nepal Tourism Board" only after the Board has approved the wording in writing. The Board may refer to Saalik in its own communications.

## 12. Ending the pilot early

Either party may end the pilot with 14 days' written notice. Saalik will deliver the data and texts described in section 9 for the period completed.

## 13. Nature of this memorandum

This memorandum records the parties' intentions for a time-limited pilot. It does not create a partnership, an exclusive arrangement or any commitment beyond the term. Any commercial arrangement after the pilot will be the subject of a separate agreement.
